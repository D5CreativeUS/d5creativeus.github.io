Privacy Policy – D5 Lisa

Effective November 22, 2026

D5 Lisa is a private email assistant built and operated by D5 Creative for its own internal use. It is used only by D5's owner, on Gmail accounts D5 owns. It is not available to the public.

What D5 Lisa accesses

With the account owner's permission through Google sign-in, D5 Lisa can:

  • Read email messages, to summarize them and count what needs attention.
  • Create draft replies, which the owner reviews and sends himself.

D5 Lisa does not send email, delete email or change account settings. Folders the owner marks as excluded are never read.

How the data is used and stored

  • Email data is used only to provide the assistant features above to the account owner.
  • It is processed and stored on D5's own computer and backup drives.
  • It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

D5 Lisa's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The account owner can remove D5 Lisa's access at any time in Google Account settings, under Security, Third-party apps with account access. Stored email data can be deleted on request to the contact below.
